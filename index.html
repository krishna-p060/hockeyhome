Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Hockey Home is designed with privacy at its core.

🔒 We Don't Collect Your Data

Hockey Home does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. All game data is stored locally on your device.

📱 What We Access

The app requires camera access to enable real-time object detection for gameplay. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, transmitted, or stored.

🎮 GameKit Integration

If you choose to use multiplayer features or leaderboards, Apple's GameKit handles authentication through your Apple ID. We do not receive or store any personal information from this process. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for more information.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Safe for Kids

Hockey Home is designed to be safe for young players. We do not include any advertising, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking. There is no chat functionality or user-generated content that could expose children to inappropriate material.
